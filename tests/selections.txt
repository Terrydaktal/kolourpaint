An obscure testcase with the selection tool is:

1. RMB-drag to create a selection and popup a menu.
2. LMB-drag outside the menu, to close it.
3. While still holding down the LMB, hold down the RMB and continue
   moving the mouse.
4. Let go of the LMB and continue moving the mouse.

etc.

KolourPaint should not crash or anything like that.

Selection borders when zoomed out:

1. Create a canvas with a solid background and a contrasting image or text.
2. Set zoom to 25%, 33%, 50%, 67%, then 75% in turn. At each zoom, draw a
   rectangular selection at several slightly different starting positions.
3. Check all four edges while drawing and after releasing the mouse. The
   dashed outline must remain continuous; no edge should disappear when its
   document pixels fall between the displayed pixels.
4. Repeat with elliptical, free-form and text selections. Unfinished free-form
   outlines must remain open. Finished curved selections must keep both their
   shape outline and bounding rectangle, with resize handles above the border.
5. Move and resize each selection repeatedly, and scroll it partially off the
   viewport. Cover and uncover the window to trigger clipped repaints. Outlines
   must match a full redraw and leave no stale pixels at previous positions.
6. Deselect, then check that no outline or handles remain. Save or copy the
   image and check that selection decorations are not part of its pixels.
7. Repeat at normal and HiDPI display scales, on light and dark canvases. At
   100% and higher zoom, the existing document-pixel border style is unchanged.
